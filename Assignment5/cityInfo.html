My Favorite Destination: Shri Ayodhya Nagari

Intro History Attractions Visit

Introduction

Shri Ayodhya Ji is known for the birth place of Lord Shri Ram, the seventh incarnation of God Vishnu. Situated on the banks of the holy Saryu River in Uttar Pradesh, India, it is one of the seven most sacred cities (Sapta Puri) for Hindus. The city holds immense spiritual and historical significance, attracting millions of devotees and tourists annually.

History

Historically, Ayodhya, also known as Saketa, was the capital of the ancient Kosala kingdom. It is mentioned extensively in the Hindu epic **Ramayana**, where it is described as a magnificent and prosperous city. Archaeological findings suggest a long, continuous history. The city's recent history is marked by the construction of the **Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple**, a monumental event signifying a new era for the ancient city.

Top Attractions

Key attractions include the newly inaugurated Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple, the serene Kanak Bhawan (believed to be a gift to Devi Sita from Kaikeyi), the spiritual ghats of the Saryu River like Ram ki Paidi, the ancient Hanuman Garhi temple, and the beautiful Dashrath Mahal. The city's food streets offer local Awadhi cuisine.

Plan Your Visit

The best seasons to visit are from October to March, when the weather is pleasant. Travel is convenient via the Maharishi Valmiki International Airport and Ayodhya Dham Junction railway station. When visiting, local **etiquette** includes dressing modestly, especially at temples, removing footwear before entering, and showing respect for religious practices.
